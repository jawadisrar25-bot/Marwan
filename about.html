About Marwan

Leadership shaped by service, standards and growth.

Marwan Aljabali is an Executive Chef based in Riyadh, with a career spanning hotels, restaurants, catering, banquets, flight catering, healthcare food service and pre-opening operations in Saudi Arabia and Jordan.

Executive profile

More than two decades in professional kitchens.

His experience covers luxury hotels, high-volume catering, café and restaurant operations, large banquet departments, outdoor catering, flight catering and healthcare food service. He has led teams of up to approximately 75 staff and has repeatedly been entrusted with menu development, food-cost management, supplier quality, payroll and vacation planning, staff development and operational readiness.

Marwan is particularly experienced in working with engineers, suppliers, HR and operational stakeholders during pre-opening phases, translating culinary requirements into practical layouts, equipment plans, production flows, menus, recipes and launch standards.

Professional philosophy

Creative food. Disciplined operations.

His approach balances guest experience with operational reality: quality, consistency, efficiency, people development and cost control must work together.

“My leadership approach combines culinary creativity with disciplined cost control, staff development, product quality and operational readiness.”Professional philosophy
